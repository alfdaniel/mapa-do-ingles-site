Tema 2 · Nível B1

Past perfect

Eu já tinha feito…

Velocidade do áudio
Past
perfectjá tinha feitohad + particípio

1A forma

I had finished. · She hadn't eaten.

Had you seen it before?

had para todos · contração: I'd

2O passado do passado

When I arrived, the movie had started. já tinha começado

A ação mais antiga vai para o past perfect.

3Compare

When I arrived, she left.ela saiu quando cheguei
When I arrived, she had left.ela já tinha saído

4Never · already · before

I had never seen snow before that trip.

She had already gone home.

Experiência até um ponto do passado.

5Because · by the time

I was tired because I had worked all day.

By the time we got there, they had eaten.

by the time = quando, até o momento em que

6Atenção ao I'd

I'd goneI had gone
I'd goI would go

Depois de I'd: particípio = had · base = would.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

When I arrived, she already left.→…she had already left.
I had went.→I had gone.
I have never seen snow before 2010.→I had never seen snow before 2010.
Did you had eaten?→Had you eaten?
I hadn't saw it.→I hadn't seen it.

lightbulb Precisa mesmo?

Se a ordem está clara (before, after), o simple também serve:

After I finished, I left. = After I had finished, I left.

Use o past perfect quando a ordem não está óbvia.
