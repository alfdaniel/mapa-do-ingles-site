Tema 3 · Nível B1

Both, either, neither

os dois, um ou outro, nenhum

Velocidade do áudio
Both
either · neitherfalando de dois2 coisas ou pessoas

1Both = os dois

Both my sisters live in Rio.

I like both. · Both of us work here.

Verbo no plural.

2Either = qualquer um

You can sit in either chair.

Either Monday or Friday is fine.

3Neither = nenhum dos dois

Neither answer is correct.

Neither Ana nor Leo came.

Já é negativo: neither… not

4Both … and

She speaks both English and Spanish.

Os dois ao mesmo tempo.

5Com of + pronome

both of usnós dois
either of themqualquer um deles
neither of younenhum de vocês

6Mais de dois?

2 itensboth · either · neither
3 ou maisall · any · none

All my friends came. · None of them called.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

The both are good.→Both are good.
Neither of them didn't come.→Neither of them came.
Both of they→Both of them
Either Monday and Friday→Either Monday or Friday
Neither of my 5 friends→None of my 5 friends

lightbulb Resposta curta

Which one? — Both.os dois
— Either.qualquer um
— Neither.nenhum

Pronúncia: “ái-der” ou “í-der”, os dois estão certos.
