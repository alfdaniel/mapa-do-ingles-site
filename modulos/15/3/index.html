Tema 3 · Nível B2

Have something done

Mandar fazer

Velocidade do áudio
Have it
donemandar fazerhave + objeto + particípio

1A ideia

I cut my hair. eu mesmo cortei

I had my hair cut. cortei no salão

Alguém fez para você.

2A forma

haveverbo
my carobjeto
washedparticípio

have muda de tempo: had, will have, am having…

3Serviços comuns

have my nails donefazer as unhas
have the car fixedconsertar o carro
have a tooth pulledarrancar um dente
have the house paintedpintar a casa

4Get (informal)

I got my hair cut yesterday.

We need to get the roof fixed.

get = have, mais coloquial.

5Coisas ruins

She had her bag stolen. roubaram a bolsa dela

Algo aconteceu com você, sem querer.

6Perguntas

Where do you have your hair cut?

When did you get your car washed?

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I cut my hair. (no salão)→I had my hair cut.
I had cut my hair at the salon.→I had my hair cut at the salon.
I had my car wash.→I had my car washed.
I got fixed my phone.→I got my phone fixed.
She stole her bag.→She had her bag stolen.

lightbulb A ordem importa

I had my car fixed.mandei consertar
I had fixed my car.eu tinha consertado

Objeto no meio = serviço. Objeto no fim = past perfect.
