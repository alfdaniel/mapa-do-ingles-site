Tema 2 · Nível B2

Passiva em todos os tempos

está sendo feito, já foi feito, será feito

Velocidade do áudio
Passiva
em todo temposó o be mudabe + particípio

1A regra

O be vai para o tempo certo. O particípio não muda.

is made · was made · will be made · has been made

2Tabela

presenteis cleaned
contínuois being cleaned
passadowas cleaned
perfecthas been cleaned
futurowill be cleaned

3Contínuo

The road is being repaired. está sendo consertada

My car was being washed. estava sendo lavado

4Perfect

The bill has been paid. já foi paga

Has the package been delivered?

5Futuro

The results will be announced tomorrow.

The building is going to be demolished.

6Com modais

It can be done. pode ser feito

This must be signed.

It should be fixed today.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

The road is repairing.→The road is being repaired.
It has been pay.→It has been paid.
It can be do.→It can be done.
It will be finish tomorrow.→It will be finished tomorrow.
The package was deliver?→Was the package delivered?

lightbulb Avisos e placas

No smoking allowed.proibido fumar
Tickets must be shown.apresente o ingresso
Pets are not allowed.proibido animais

A passiva é muito comum em avisos e notícias.
