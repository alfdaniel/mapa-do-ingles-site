Tema 1 · Nível B2

Passiva: presente e passado

é feito · foi feito

Velocidade do áudio
Passive
voiceé feito · foi feitobe + particípio

1Ativa × passiva

Shakespeare wrote Hamlet. ativa

Hamlet was written by Shakespeare. passiva

O foco vai para o objeto.

2Presente

Coffee is grown in Brazil.

English is spoken here.

is/are + particípio: fatos e processos.

3Passado

The house was built in 1950.

My car was stolen! roubaram meu carro

was/were + particípio

4Quando usar

Quem fez não importa ou não se sabe:

My phone was stolen.

The bridge was closed yesterday.

5By = por

The book was written by a Brazilian.

Use by só se a informação for importante.

6Negativa e pergunta

It wasn't made in China.

Was it made in Brazil?

Where were you born?

Erros comuns de brasileiros

The house was build in 1950.→The house was built in 1950.
English is speak here.→English is spoken here.
My car stole.→My car was stolen.
It made in China.→It was made in China.
I born in 1990.→I was born in 1990.

lightbulb O “se” e o “eles” do português

O português usa “se” ou “eles” sem sujeito:

Roubaram meu carro. → My car was stolen.

Fala-se inglês aqui. → English is spoken here.
