Look, turn, come e outros

Mais phrasal verbs do dia a dia

Velocidade do áudio
Look · turn
comemais phrasal verbsverbo + partícula

1Look

look forprocurar
look aftercuidar de
look uppesquisar (palavra)
look forward toaguardar ansioso
look out!cuidado!

2Turn

turn on / offligar / desligar

turn downrecusar (oferta)
turn intovirar, transformar-se

3Come

come backvoltar
come inentrar
come acrossencontrar por acaso
come up withter (uma ideia)

4Go

go outsair (à noite)
go offtocar (alarme)

5Find, work, run…

6Em frases

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I'm looking my keys.→I'm looking for my keys.
Open the light.→Turn on the light.
I discovered out the truth.→I found out the truth.
We finished the milk. (acabou)→We ran out of milk.
Turn down the volume. (aumentar)→Turn up the volume.
