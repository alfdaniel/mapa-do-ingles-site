Tema 1 · Nível B2

Get, take, put

Os phrasal verbs mais usados

Velocidade do áudio
Get · take
putphrasal verbsverbo + partícula

1O que é?

Verbo + partícula = sentido novo:

get up = levantar · give up = desistir

Não traduza palavra por palavra!

2Get

get uplevantar (da cama)
get on / offsubir / descer (ônibus)
get along (with)se dar bem
get oversuperar
get backvoltar

3Take

take offtirar (roupa) · decolar
take upcomeçar (hobby)
take afterpuxar a, parecer com
take care ofcuidar de

4Put

put onvestir
put offadiar
put up withtolerar
put awayguardar

5Get sozinho

receberI got a message.
ficarI got tired.
chegarI got home at 8.
entenderI don't get it.

6Em frases

The plane took off at 9.

I can't put up with this noise!

She takes after her mother.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I got up of the bed.→I got up.
Get in the bus.→Get on the bus.
I can't put up this noise.→I can't put up with this noise.
She takes after to her mom.→She takes after her mom.
I finished my sadness.→I got over it.

lightbulb in × on no transporte

get in / out ofcar, taxi
get on / offbus, train, plane, bike

A mesma lógica do in the car × on the bus.
