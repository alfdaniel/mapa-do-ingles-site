Tema 1 · Nível B1

Present perfect

Experiências e o passado que chega até agora

Velocidade do áudio
Present
perfectjá · nunca · desdehave + particípio

1A forma

I / you / we / theyhave worked · I've
he / she / ithas worked · she's

She hasn't called. · Have you eaten?

2Experiências na vida

I've been to Chile. Já fui ao Chile.

Have you ever tried sushi? Você já…?

I've never seen snow.

3Already · yet · just

alreadyjá (afirmativa)
yetainda (neg.) · já (perg.)
justacabei de

I've just arrived. · Not yet.

4For × since

forduração: for 3 years
sinceinício: since 2020

I've lived here since 2018.

Em português: “há 3 anos”, “desde 2020”.

5Perfect × passado simples

quando? não importaI've been to Paris.
quando? importaI went in 2019.

Com yesterday, ago, in 2019: passado simples.

6Been × gone

She's been to Rome. foi e voltou

She's gone to Rome. foi e está lá

Para experiências, use been.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I live here since 2020.→I've lived here since 2020.
Have you went?→Have you gone?
I have seen him yesterday.→I saw him yesterday.
I've been in Paris. (viagem)→I've been to Paris.
since three years→for three years

lightbulb O português pensa diferente

Moro aqui há 5 anos.I've lived here for 5 years.
Trabalho aqui desde 2019.I've worked here since 2019.

Em português usamos o presente. Em inglês, o present perfect.
