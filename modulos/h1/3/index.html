Números grandes e porcentagens

thousand, million, percent, half…

Velocidade do áudio
Big
numbersnúmeros grandesthousand · million

1Centenas e milhares

100a / one hundred
1,000a / one thousand
1,000,000a / one million
1,000,000,000a / one billion (bilhão)

2Sem plural

two hundred hundreds

five thousand people

Mas: hundreds of people (centenas de)

3Lendo números

2,345 → two thousand, three hundred and forty-five

O britânico usa “and”; o americano às vezes omite.

4Porcentagens e decimais

25% → twenty-five percent

1.5 → one point five

50% off → 50% de desconto

Erros comuns de brasileiros

two hundreds→two hundred
five thousands people→five thousand people
one comma five→one point five
a half hour→half an hour
hundred of people→hundreds of people
