Restaurante, loja e farmácia

Pedir, provar, pagar e explicar o que sente

1Restaurante: pedir

A table for two, please.mesa para dois
Can I see the menu?o cardápio
I'll have the…vou querer…

2Restaurante: pagar

Can we have the check?a conta (EUA)
the billa conta (UK)
Is service included?serviço incluso?
Keep the change.fique com o troco

3Loja

I'm just looking.só olhando
Do you have this in M?tem no tamanho M?
Can I try it on?posso provar?
Where's the fitting room?o provador

4Pagar

Cash or card?dinheiro ou cartão?
Can I pay by card?no cartão
Can I pay in installments?parcelado
Can I get a receipt?recibo, nota

5Farmácia

I have a headache.dor de cabeça
I have a cold / the flu.resfriado / gripe
Do I need a prescription?receita
How often should I take it?de quanto em quanto tempo

6Educação

Use Can I have / Could I get + please.

I want a coffee. → Could I get a coffee, please?

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I want the account.→Can I have the check?
Can I prove it?→Can I try it on?
I'm only seeing.→I'm just looking.
Give me a coffee.→Can I get a coffee, please?
I have pain of head.→I have a headache.
