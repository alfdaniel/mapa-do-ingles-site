Erros comuns de brasileiros

I want go.→I want to go.
I came here for study.→I came here to study.
I want that you come.→I want you to come.
I decided go.→I decided to go.
I can't afford buy it.→I can't afford to buy it.

lightbulb “Para” = to

Para indicar objetivo, use to (não for):

I'm saving money to travel.

for + substantivo: I'm saving for a trip.
