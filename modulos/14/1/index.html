4Depois de preposição

Sempre -ing:

I'm good at cooking.

Thanks for coming!

She left without saying goodbye.

5-ing como sujeito

Swimming is good for you.

Learning English takes time.

Todas pedem -ing depois.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I enjoy to cook.→I enjoy cooking.
Do you mind to wait?→Do you mind waiting?
I look forward to see you.→I look forward to seeing you.
Swim is good.→Swimming is good.
Thanks for come.→Thanks for coming.

lightbulb look forward to + -ing

O “to” aqui é preposição, não infinitivo:

I look forward to hearing from you.

Muito usado no fim de e-mails.
