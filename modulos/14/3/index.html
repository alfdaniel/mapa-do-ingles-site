Tema 3 · Nível B1

Os que mudam de sentido

stop, remember, try, make, let

Velocidade do áudio
Stop · try
remembero sentido muda-ing × to

1Stop

stop smokingparar de fumar
stop to smokeparar para fumar

2Remember / forget

remember lockinglembro de ter trancado
remember to locklembrar de trancar

forget funciona igual.

3Try

try to opententar (esforço)
try openingexperimentar (teste)

4Os dois servem

like, love, hate, start: sentido igual.

I like swimming. = I like to swim.

It started raining / to rain.

5Regret / need

regret sayingme arrependo de ter dito
need to fixpreciso consertar
needs fixingprecisa ser consertado

6Make e let (sem to)

She made me cry. me fez chorar

Let me help. deixa eu ajudar

made me to cry · let me to

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I stopped to smoke. (larguei)→I stopped smoking.
Remember locking the door! (tarefa)→Remember to lock the door!
She made me to wait.→She made me wait.
Let me to help.→Let me help.
I forgot meeting him. (não fui)→I forgot to meet him.

lightbulb Dica de memória

-ing olha para trás (algo que já aconteceu).

to olha para frente (algo a fazer).

I remember meeting her. ← · Remember to call her! →
