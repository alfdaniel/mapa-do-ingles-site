Tema 3 · Nível A1–A2

Pronomes e posse

me, my, mine e o ’s

Velocidade do áudio
Pronomes
e possede quem é?I · me · my · mine

1A tabela completa

Ime · my · mine
youyou · your · yours
hehim · his · his
sheher · her · hers
itit · its · —
weus · our · ours
theythem · their · theirs

2Objeto: me, him…

Depois do verbo ou da preposição:

She loves me. Ela me ama.

Call him. · Come with us.

Em inglês vem depois do verbo.

3my × mine

This is my car. + substantivo

This car is mine. sozinho

mine = o meu, a minha.

4his × her

Depende do dono, não da coisa:

Ana and her brother

Pedro and his sister

“seu/sua” em português confunde.

5O ’s de posse

Ana's car o carro da Ana

my parents' house plural com s: só o ’

Evite the car of Ana.

6Whose?

Whose phone is this? De quem é?

It's Lucas's. · It's mine.

Não confunda com who's = who is.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I love she.→I love her.
the car of Ana→Ana's car
Ana and his brother→Ana and her brother
This is mine car.→This is my car.
The dog ate it's food.→The dog ate its food.

lightbulb its × it's

it's= it is: It's cold.
its= dele (coisa): its food

Teste: dá para trocar por “it is”? Então leva apóstrofo.
