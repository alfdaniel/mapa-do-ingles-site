Casa e rotina

stay up, sleep in, clean up…

Velocidade do áudio
Casa
e rotinaphrasal verbsem casa

1Acordar e dormir

wake upacordar
get uplevantar
stay upficar acordado até tarde
doze offcochilar
sleep indormir até mais tarde

2Limpeza

clean uparrumar, limpar
tidy uporganizar
wipe offlimpar (com pano)
throw outjogar fora

3Cozinha

heat upesquentar
chop uppicar
boil overtransbordar fervendo
eat outcomer fora

4Roupas

try onexperimentar
dress upse arrumar (chique)
hang uppendurar · desligar
take offtirar

5Casa e mudança

move in / outmudar para / sair de
settle inse adaptar
fix upreformar
set upmontar, instalar

6Em frases

I stayed up until 2 am.

Can you heat up the soup?

We moved in last month.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I awaked up at 7.→I woke up at 7.
Can I try this shirt?→Can I try on this shirt?
We moved inside last week.→We moved in last week.
I slept late. (fiquei acordado)→I stayed up late.
Hang off the phone.→Hang up the phone.

lightbulb stay up × sleep in

stay upficar acordado até tarde (noite)
sleep indormir até tarde (manhã)

I stayed up late on Friday, so I slept in on Saturday.
