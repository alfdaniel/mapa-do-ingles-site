Tema 2 · Nível B2

Dedução no passado

deve ter · pode ter · não pode ter

Velocidade do áudio
Must have
can't havedeve ter · não pode terdedução + have + partic.

1Must have = deve ter

The street is wet. It must have rained.

Quase certeza, com evidência.

2Can't have = não pode ter

She can't have left. Her bag is here.

couldn't have = o mesmo

3Might / may have = pode ter

He might have missed the bus.

She may have forgotten.

Uns 50% de certeza.

4Escala

must havedeve ter (quase certeza)
might / may / could havepode ter (talvez)
can't / couldn't haveimpossível

5Não confunda

must have donededução
had to doobrigação no passado

I had to work. I must have worked

6Respostas curtas

Where's Leo? — He might have gone home.

Who ate the cake? — It must have been the kids!

Erros comuns de brasileiros

He must went home.→He must have gone home.
She can't has left.→She can't have left.
I must have worked yesterday. (obrigação)→I had to work yesterday.
He mustn't have seen it. (dedução)→He can't have seen it.
Maybe he might have…→He might have…

lightbulb mustn't ≠ can't have

Para dedução negativa, use can't have, não mustn't:

He can't have done it. He was with me!

mustn't = proibição.
