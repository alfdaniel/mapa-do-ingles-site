Tema 2 · Nível B2

Where, whose, when

onde, cujo, quando

Velocidade do áudio
Where · whose
whenonde · cujo · quandolugar · posse · tempo

1Where = lugar

The city where I was born.

The café where we met.

= in which

2Whose = cujo

The girl whose dad is a pilot.

A man whose car was stolen.

Posse: a menina cujo pai…

3When = tempo

The day when we met.

2020 was the year when everything changed.

4Why = motivo

The reason why I'm late.

the reason why = o motivo pelo qual

5Where × which

The hotel where we stayed. ficamos lá

The hotel which we booked. reservamos o hotel

Teste: cabe um “lá”? → where

6Whose × who's

whoseposse: whose car
who'swho is: Who's there?

Erros comuns de brasileiros

The city that I was born→The city where I was born
The man who his car…→The man whose car…
The day where we met→The day when we met
The hotel where we booked→The hotel (that) we booked
Who's bag is this?→Whose bag is this?

lightbulb “Cujo” sem medo

O português quase não usa “cujo” na fala. O inglês usa whose o tempo todo:

I have a friend whose sister is an actress.

= Tenho uma amiga que a irmã é atriz.

Leu o mapa em voz alta? Agora pratique sem olhar.
