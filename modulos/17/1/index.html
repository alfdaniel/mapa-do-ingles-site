Who, which, that

O “que” das orações

Velocidade do áudio
Who · which
thatque · quemsubstantivo + who/which

1Who = pessoas

The woman who lives next door is a doctor.

a mulher que mora ao lado

2Which = coisas

The car which I bought is red.

o carro que eu comprei

3That = os dois

The man that called… · The book that I read…

Mais comum na fala.

4Não repita o pronome

The book that I bought it

The book that I bought. ✔

5Juntar frases

I have a friend. She speaks 5 languages.

→ I have a friend who speaks 5 languages.

6Preposição no fim

The house that I live in. onde moro

The person who I talked to.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

The man which called you→The man who called you
The book what I read→The book that I read
The car that I bought it→The car that I bought
People who lives here→People who live here
The girl that her name is Ana→The girl whose name is Ana

lightbulb O “que” tem 3 traduções

pessoawho / that
coisawhich / that
na dúvidathat

what nunca é o “que” relativo!
