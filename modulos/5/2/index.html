Tema 2 · Nível A2

Passado simples + did

O que você fez

Velocidade do áudio
Past
simplefiz · fui · comi-ed · did + base

1Afirmativa (-ed)

work → worked+ ed
live → lived+ d
study → studiedconsoante + y → ied
stop → stoppedCVC: dobra

Igual para todas as pessoas.

2Negativa

I didn't work yesterday.

She didn't call me.

O did já é passado. O verbo volta à base: didn't worked

3Pergunta

Did you watch the game?

Yes, I did. · No, I didn't.

What did you do last night?

4Pronúncia do -ed

/t/worked, stopped, watched
/d/played, lived, called
/ɪd/wanted, needed, visited

/ɪd/ só depois de t ou d.

5Pergunta sobre o sujeito

Sem did quando who/what é o sujeito:

Who called you? Quem te ligou?

What happened? O que aconteceu?

6be × did

adjetivo / lugarwas/were: I was tired.
outro verbodid: I didn't sleep.

Nunca os dois juntos: Did you were

Erros comuns de brasileiros

Did you went?→Did you go?
I didn't worked.→I didn't work.
She studyed.→She studied.
Who did call you?→Who called you?
I was work yesterday.→I worked yesterday.

lightbulb O -ed não é “éd”

O brasileiro tende a falar “uôrkéd”. O certo:

worked“uôrkt”: uma sílaba
played“plêid”: uma sílaba
wanted“uón-tid”: sílaba extra

Só ganha sílaba extra depois de t ou d.
