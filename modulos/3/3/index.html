Tema 3 · Nível A1–A2

Present continuous

O agora e os compromissos marcados

Velocidade do áudio
Present
continuousacontecendo agorabe + -ing

1A forma

Iam working
he / she / itis working
you / we / theyare working

She isn't working. · Are you working?

2Agora / esta fase

I'm eating now. agora

She's studying a lot this week. fase

now, right now, at the moment, today

3Grafia do -ing

workingregra geral
make → makingtira o -e
run → runningCVC: dobra
lie → lying-ie → -y

CVC = consoante + vogal + consoante.

4Futuro marcado

I'm meeting Ana tomorrow. combinado

We're flying to Lisbon on Friday.

Tem data, hora ou pessoa combinada.

5Horário fixo = presente

The train leaves at 8.

The movie starts at 9 pm.

Ônibus, voo, aula, filme: agenda pública.

6Verbos de estado

Esses não vão para o -ing:

know · like · loveI'm knowing
want · needI'm needing
understandI'm understanding

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I working now.→I'm working now.
She is work.→She is working.
I'm liking it.→I like it.
Are you understand?→Do you understand?
I'm runing.→I'm running.
