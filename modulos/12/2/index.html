Tema 2 · Nível B1

Marcadores do perfect

lately, recently, so far, still, yet…

Velocidade do áudio
Lately
so faraté agora+ present perfect

1Lately / recently

I've been tired lately. ultimamente

Have you seen Ana recently? recentemente

2So far

So far, I've visited 10 countries. até agora

Uma contagem até este momento.

So far, so good! Até agora, tudo bem!

3Período aberto

I've had 3 coffees today. o dia não acabou

I had 3 coffees yesterday. acabou → simple

4Already · yet · still

alreadyjá (antes do esperado)
yetainda (neg.) · já (perg.) · no fim
stillainda (continua)

5Still + negativa

I still haven't finished. ainda não terminei

She's still working. ainda está trabalhando

still vem antes de haven't.

6Ever · for ages

It's the best pizza I've ever had.

I haven't seen him for ages. há séculos

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I didn't finish yet.→I haven't finished yet.
I've seen her yesterday.→I saw her yesterday.
I've already finished it last week.→I finished it last week.
I yet haven't eaten.→I haven't eaten yet.
Lately I go to the gym.→Lately I've been going to the gym.

lightbulb Período aberto × fechado

today, this week, so farperfect
yesterday, last week, in 2020simple

Se o período ainda está aberto, use o perfect.
