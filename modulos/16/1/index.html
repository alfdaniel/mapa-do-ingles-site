Reported statements

Ele disse que…

Velocidade do áudio
Reported
speechele disse que…um tempo para trás

1A ideia

“I'm tired.” direto

She said (that) she was tired. indireto

O verbo recua um tempo.

2O recuo

am / iswas
do / doesdid
did / have donehad done
willwould
cancould

3Pronomes mudam

“I love my job.”

He said he loved his job.

Ajuste pela pessoa que conta.

4Tempo e lugar

todaythat day
tomorrowthe next day
yesterdaythe day before
herethere
nowthen

5Sem recuo

Se ainda é verdade, pode manter:

She said she lives in Rio. ainda mora

He says he is busy. say no presente

6That é opcional

He said (that) he was late.

Na fala, o that quase sempre some.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

She said me that…→She told me that…
He said that he is tired yesterday.→He said that he was tired.
She said: that she was…→She said that she was…
He said he will come.→He said he would come.
She said she can't.→She said she couldn't.

lightbulb A gramática da fofoca

O reported speech é o que você usa para contar novidades 😄

Guess what? Ana said she was moving to Canada!

And she told me she had quit her job!
