Reported questions

Ela perguntou se… / onde…

Velocidade do áudio
Reported
questionsperguntou se…ask + if/wh + S + V

1Sim/não → if

“Are you tired?”

She asked if I was tired.

if ou whether = se

2Wh: mantém a palavra

“Where do you live?”

He asked where I lived.

Ordem de afirmativa: sujeito + verbo.

3Sem do/does/did

“What did you buy?”

She asked what I had bought. what did I buy

4Pergunta educada

Do you know where the bank is?

Could you tell me what time it opens?

Mesma ordem: sujeito + verbo.

5Wonder = será que

I wonder if she's OK. Será que ela está bem?

I wonder why he left.

6Ponto final

She asked where I lived.

Não usa ponto de interrogação no reported.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

She asked where did I live.→She asked where I lived.
He asked me do I like it.→He asked me if I liked it.
Do you know where is the bank?→Do you know where the bank is?
She asked if was I OK.→She asked if I was OK.
I wonder what time is it.→I wonder what time it is.
