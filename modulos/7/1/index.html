Tema 1 · Nível A2

In, on, at

Preposições de tempo e lugar

Velocidade do áudio
In · on · attempo e lugardo maior ao menor

1Tempo: a pirâmide

inmês, ano: in May, in 2020
ondia, data: on Monday
athora: at 7 pm

Do maior (in) ao mais exato (at).

2Lugar

indentro: in the box, in Brazil
onsuperfície: on the table
atponto: at the door

3Expressões de tempo

in the morningde manhã
in the afternoonà tarde
at nightà noite
on the weekendno fim de semana (US)

4Transporte

incar, taxi: in the car
onbus, train, plane
bymeio: by car, by bus

Dá para ficar em pé lá dentro? → on

5Lugares fixos com at

at home · at work · at school

at the airport · at the party

Mas país e cidade: at Brazil → in Brazil

6Movimento

topara: go to work
intopara dentro: into the car
fromde: from Brazil
out ofpara fora

Sem to: go home, go downtown.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

in Monday→on Monday
at May→in May
I'm in home.→I'm at home.
I go to home.→I go home.
in the bus→on the bus

lightbulb Sem preposição

Com last, next, this, every, tomorrow, yesterday:

I saw her last week. in last week

See you next Monday. on next Monday

I go there every day.
