Tema 3 · Nível A2

Much, many e quantidade

Muito, pouco, demais e suficiente

Velocidade do áudio
Much
manyquanto?contável × incontável

1Much × many

manycontável: many friends
muchincontável: much time
a lot ofos dois: a lot of time

Na afirmativa, a lot of soa mais natural.

2Perguntas

How many people? quantas pessoas?

How much water? quanta água?

How much is it? quanto custa?

3Pouco

a fewcontável: a few friends
a littleincontável: a little sugar

a few / a little = um pouco (positivo)

few / little = pouco (negativo)

4Too = demais

It's too expensive. caro demais

too much sugar · too many cars

too indica um problema.

5Enough = suficiente

I have enough money. antes do substantivo

It's big enough. depois do adjetivo

He's not old enough to drive.

6Very × too

very hotmuito quente (informação)
too hotquente demais (problema)

The soup is too hot. I can't eat it.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

many money→a lot of money
How much people?→How many people?
It's too good!→It's very good!
enough big→big enough
I have few money.→I have little money.

lightbulb Dica rápida

Dá para contar (1, 2, 3)? → many, a few, how many

Não dá para contar? → much, a little, how much

Na dúvida: a lot of funciona com os dois.
