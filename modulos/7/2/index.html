Tema 2 · Nível A2

Comparativos e superlativos

Maior, melhor, o mais…

Velocidade do áudio
-er · -estcompararmore · most

1Curtas: -er / -est

cheapcheaper · the cheapest
bigbigger · the biggest
easyeasier · the easiest
nicenicer · the nicest

CVC dobra · y → i · final e: só r/st

2Longas: more / most

expensivemore … · the most …
interestingmore … · the most …
comfortablemore … · the most …

2 sílabas ou mais (sem -y): more / most.

3Irregulares

goodbetter · the best
badworse · the worst
farfarther · the farthest
much / manymore · the most

4Than = do que

Rio is bigger than Recife.

This is more expensive than that.

Nunca juntos: more big · more better

5Superlativo: the

She's the best player on the team.

It's the most beautiful city in Brazil.

the + -est/most · in + lugar ou grupo

6as … as = tão … quanto

I'm as tall as my brother.

It's not as cheap as I thought.

Igualdade, ou uma negativa mais suave.

Erros comuns de brasileiros

more big→bigger
more better→better
bigger that→bigger than
the most cheap→the cheapest
She's the more beautiful.→She's the most beautiful.

lightbulb Atalhos úteis

much biggermuito maior
a bit cheaperum pouco mais barato
the same asigual a
different fromdiferente de

“Muito” + comparativo = much, não very: very bigger
