Tema 2 · Nível B1

-ed × -ing

bored ou boring?

Velocidade do áudio
-ed × -ingsinto × causabored · boring

1A regra

-edcomo eu me sinto
-ingo que causa o sentimento

I'm bored. The class is boring.

2Pares mais usados

bored · boringentediado · chato
tired · tiringcansado · cansativo
interested · interestinginteressado · interessante
excited · excitinganimado · emocionante

3Mais pares

surprised · surprisingsurpreso · surpreendente
confused · confusingconfuso (eu) · confuso (algo)
worried · worryingpreocupado · preocupante
amazed · amazingmaravilhado · incrível

4Pessoas com -ing

He's boring. ele é chato

He's bored. ele está entediado

Cuidado: muda tudo!

5Preposições

interested ininteressado em
excited aboutanimado com
worried aboutpreocupado com
tired ofcansado de

6Falso amigo: embarrassed

I was embarrassed. fiquei com vergonha

Grávida = pregnant! embarrassed

ashamed = envergonhado (por culpa)

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I'm boring.→I'm bored.
The movie was excited.→The movie was exciting.
I'm interesting in music.→I'm interested in music.
She's embarrassed. (grávida)→She's pregnant.
I'm tiring.→I'm tired.

lightbulb Teste rápido

Pergunte: quem sente? → -ed

O que causa? → -ing

The trip was tiring, so we were tired.
