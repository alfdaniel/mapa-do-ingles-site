Erros comuns de brasileiros

I study in a college. (ensino médio)→I go to high school.
I bought it in a library.→I bought it at a bookstore.
My parents came for Christmas. (tios)→My relatives came for Christmas.
I'll eat a lunch. (lanche)→I'll have a snack.
I watch a novel.→I watch a soap opera.
