6Por que importa

Os nativos usam combinações fixas:

heavy rain ✔ · strong rain

take a photo ✔ · make a photo

Erros comuns de brasileiros

I took breakfast.→I had breakfast.
Can I make a photo?→Can I take a photo?
strong rain→heavy rain
Make attention!→Pay attention!
I took a good time.→I had a good time.
