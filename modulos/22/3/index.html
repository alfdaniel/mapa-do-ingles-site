Erros comuns de brasileiros

It costs the eyes of the face.→It costs an arm and a leg.
It's a cake piece.→It's a piece of cake.
Kill two rabbits with one stone.→Kill two birds with one stone.
I'm on the cloud nine.→I'm on cloud nine.
Stop rolling!→Stop beating around the bush!

lightbulb Use com moderação

Idioms deixam a fala natural, mas não exagere: um ou dois por conversa.

Os mais seguros: a piece of cake, under the weather, break the ice.
