Tema 3 · Nível C1

Ênfase com do, so, such

I do like it! · It's such a nice day!

Velocidade do áudio
I do like it!ênfasedo · so · such · really

1Do de ênfase

I do like it! eu gosto, sim!

She does know. · I did call you!

Contradiz ou reforça.

2So + adjetivo

It's so hot! · She's so kind.

It was so cold that we stayed in.

3Such + substantivo

It's such a nice day!

They're such good friends.

such a + adjetivo + substantivo

4So × such

so + adjetivoso beautiful
such (a) + adj. + subst.such a beautiful place
so much / manyso many people

5Palavras de ênfase

reallyI really need it.
absolutelyabsolutely amazing
at allI don't like it at all.
the verythe very best

6Exclamações

What a view! · What nice people!

How beautiful!

what + substantivo · how + adjetivo

Erros comuns de brasileiros

It's so a nice day.→It's such a nice day.
She's such kind.→She's so kind.
I do liked it.→I did like it.
What beautiful!→How beautiful!
I don't like it nothing.→I don't like it at all.

lightbulb A voz da ênfase

No do de ênfase, a força vai no auxiliar:

I DO love you. · She DID call!

Na fala, a entonação mostra a ênfase.
